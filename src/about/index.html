About Forest Valley Apps

apps.fv.dev is the app catalog of Forest Valley — a software development company building tools for e-commerce merchants.

What We Build

Forest Valley Apps publishes specialized tools for Ecwid merchants — specifically for stores embedded on WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Joomla, and custom websites. Each app targets a specific gap that Ecwid's own platform doesn't address for embedded stores.

Our first app, Redirect & 404 Manager, lets merchants manage URL redirects, detect broken links, and auto-redirect deleted products — without requiring server access.
