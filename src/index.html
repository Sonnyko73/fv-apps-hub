Built for Ecwid Merchants

Ecwid is one of the most flexible e-commerce platforms available — you can embed it on any website, from WordPress to Wix to a fully custom build. That flexibility is powerful, but it also creates gaps that Ecwid's own tools don't address.

Ecwid's built-in features assume you're on Instant Site. The moment you embed your store on an external website, you lose access to features like custom URL slugs, server-side redirects, and platform-level 404 handling. If a product is deleted, visitors hit a dead end. If you rename a category, old URLs break silently. Google notices — and your rankings follow.

We build apps that fill those gaps. Each app targets one specific problem that Ecwid merchants on embedded stores face: broken links, lost SEO equity, missing URL control. We go deep on one thing rather than building a broad platform that does everything halfway.

Every app is designed to install in minutes with no coding required, run reliably in the background, and handle the edge cases that matter — bulk operations, conflict detection, automatic recovery when something changes on your store.

Who Our Apps Are For

WordPress + Ecwid Merchants

The most common embedded Ecwid setup. You have full control of your hosting, but Ecwid's server-level tools don't reach your WordPress site. Our apps bridge that gap.

Wix and Squarespace Stores

Platform-hosted sites with embedded Ecwid stores face the same redirect and URL management gaps — and have even less server access. Our apps work on any platform.

Merchants Migrating Products

Moving products between categories, renaming URLs, or discontinuing items? Every change is a potential 404. Our apps detect and redirect broken URLs automatically.
